The origin

Started as a repair shop. Expanded around the same customer problem.

GIE's official company history says C. H. and Ruth Raines founded the business in 1959 as a motor repair shop. The company later expanded into motor distribution, pumps, repair parts and electrical supplies while remaining family owned and operated.
