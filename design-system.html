06 / Interaction

Hover Behavior

Navigation

Underline or background transition only. Keep movement subtle and fast.

Buttons

Translate upward 2–3px and invert or lighten the background.

Cards / Badges

Use background/color inversion and a slight upward movement. Avoid complex click states for the submission prototype.

07 / Usage

Design Principles

Industrial, not generic corporate. Use condensed type, rigid grids, technical labels and hard edges.
Content first. Exact client copy must remain intact; visual hierarchy should organize it, not rewrite it.
High contrast. Navy establishes authority, amber signals action, pale blue creates information zones.
Photography should be purposeful. Prefer real industrial repair, motors, technicians and facilities over decorative stock imagery.
Prototype restraint. Hover-only interaction keeps the deliverable focused and review-safe.
