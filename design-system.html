CENNEXT / Gainesville Industrial Electric

Web Design System.

01 / Foundation + Components

A portfolio-ready reference extracted from the production prototype: industrial color, condensed typography, rigid grids, hard-edged controls and fast, purposeful interaction.

01 / Logo

Industrial mark. Compact footprint.

The GIE lockup pairs a custom rotor symbol with a condensed wordmark. The amber rotor acts as the recognisable accent while the wordmark stays authoritative in navy or paper.

GIE
Logo usage

Primary: navy wordmark + amber rotor on paper.

Reverse: paper wordmark + amber rotor on navy.

Clear space: at least one rotor width around the lockup.

Character: technical, direct, compact, no decorative rounding.

02 / Color palette

Navy authority. Amber signal.

Navy establishes trust and machinery-grade seriousness. Amber marks action and urgency. Pale blues create technical information zones, while warm paper keeps long-form content approachable.

12 / Motion principles

Fast enough for work. Visible enough for confidence.

Motion confirms intent instead of entertaining. Most transitions run around 180–280ms using the same ease-out curve as the live prototype.
