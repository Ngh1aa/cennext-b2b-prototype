03 / Architecture

The prototype moves from a brief-compliant one-page landing page into focused B2B task pages. Every branch leads toward repair confidence, a service decision or direct contact.

01 / Primary architecture

One home. Five task-oriented routes.

The navigation mirrors how an industrial buyer thinks: understand capability, evaluate recurring support, decide repair versus replacement, validate the supplier, then contact the repair team.
