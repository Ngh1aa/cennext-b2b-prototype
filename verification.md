# Mechanical visual refinement verification

The GIE wordmark is now a CSS-built rotor/gear-inspired mark paired with the GIE initials, replacing the plain diamond-only mark. The diagnostic callout no longer contains the clock/gauge icon. Decorative icons were also removed from the volume-program callout and the trust badge cards; functional arrow/check icons remain where they communicate actions or list state.

Headings now use Barlow Condensed with a heavier uppercase industrial treatment, while Manrope remains the body font and Space Mono remains the metadata/label font. Desktop preview at 1440 × 900 and mobile preview at 390 × 844 both render without heading overflow. `pnpm check` and `pnpm build` pass.
