Common failure causes

Small issues become expensive shutdowns.

Early diagnostics matter because heat, contamination, imbalance and mechanical wear often develop before a motor reaches a complete failure state.

01

Heat + bearing wear

Load conditions, lubrication problems and improper alignment can raise temperature and accelerate mechanical wear.

02

Moisture + contamination

Ingress can contribute to corrosion and insulation deterioration in demanding environments.

03

Electrical imbalance

Electrical irregularities or insulation breakdown can produce measurable changes in performance.

04

Harsh environments

Dust, chemicals and corrosive exposure can shorten component life and increase maintenance needs.

05

Deferred maintenance

Small defects become harder and more expensive to correct when scheduled maintenance is delayed.
