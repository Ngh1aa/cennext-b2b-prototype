Before you contact

Five details make the first response better.

Maintenance teams often lose time because the service provider has to ask for basic asset information before the repair conversation can start.

01

Motor ID

Manufacturer, model or nameplate details if available.

02

HP + voltage

Basic electrical and mechanical rating information.

03

Symptoms

Noise, heat, vibration, breaker trips, speed or visible damage.

04

Application

Pump, fan, conveyor, compressor or other driven equipment.

05

Deadline

When production needs the motor back in service.
